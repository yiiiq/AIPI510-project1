
GULF COAST HURRICANE VULNERABILITY ANALYSIS - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SCOPE:
• 534 counties across 5 Gulf Coast states
• Focus on evacuation barriers, vulnerable populations, and hurricane preparedness

KEY FINDINGS:

1. EVACUATION CHALLENGES:
• 38.2% of counties (204) face severe evacuation barriers
• 11.8% of counties have populations with limited vehicle access
• 19.7% of counties have significant mobile home populations

2. VULNERABLE POPULATIONS:
• 50.4% of counties have high concentrations of vulnerable households
• 4.5% of counties face significant medical vulnerability challenges
• Most common vulnerability: Uninsur

3. HURRICANE RISK PROFILE:
• 137 counties classified as very high risk
• 181 counties classified as high risk
• Worst-case counties (high evacuation barriers + medical vulnerability): 8

4. STATE COMPARISONS:
• Highest average risk: Louisiana (0.708)
• Lowest average risk: Alabama (0.578)

5. CRITICAL INSIGHT:
• Minority Language poses the greatest challenge across the Gulf Coast
• 5 counties face 5 or more concurrent vulnerability factors

RECOMMENDATION: Priority should be given to the 137 counties with
Hurricane Risk Index scores above 0.8, focusing on evacuation planning and medical preparedness.
